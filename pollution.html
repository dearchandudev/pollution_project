TAKE ACTION

Detailed Pollution Solutions

01

🌫️ Reduce Air Pollution

  • Use public transport whenever practical.
  • Walk or cycle for short distances.
  • Avoid unnecessary vehicle idling.
  • Maintain vehicles properly.
  • Do not burn leaves or household waste.
  • Control construction dust.
  • Industries should use appropriate emission controls.
Example: Compost dry leaves instead of burning them.
02

💧 Protect Water

  • Treat sewage before discharge.
  • Treat industrial wastewater.
  • Never throw plastic into rivers.
  • Reduce chemical runoff.
  • Protect lakes and wetlands.
  • Use rainwater harvesting.
  • Dispose of oil and chemicals responsibly.
Example: Install a rainwater harvesting system in a school.
03

🛍️ Reduce Plastic

  • Carry reusable shopping bags.
  • Use reusable water bottles.
  • Avoid unnecessary single-use plastic.
  • Separate recyclable waste.
  • Reuse containers where appropriate.
  • Support better packaging choices.
Example: Replace disposable bottles with a reusable bottle.
04

🏭 Cleaner Industry

  • Install suitable pollution-control equipment.
  • Treat wastewater.
  • Monitor emissions.
  • Improve energy efficiency.
  • Use cleaner energy where practical.
  • Handle hazardous waste correctly.
  • Reuse process water where feasible.
Example: Recycle process water after appropriate treatment.
05

🌱 Protect Soil

  • Use fertilizers responsibly.
  • Avoid unnecessary pesticides.
  • Compost organic waste.
  • Never dump batteries or e-waste on land.
  • Test soil when appropriate.
  • Maintain vegetation.
Example: Separate electronic waste from household waste.
06

🔊 Reduce Noise

  • Avoid unnecessary honking.
  • Maintain vehicle silencers.
  • Control construction noise.
  • Use loudspeakers responsibly.
  • Protect quiet zones.
  • Monitor excessive community noise.
Example: Avoid unnecessary horn use near schools and hospitals.
